1A mathematics teacher with an engineer's habits

I teach calculus, algebra, coordinate geometry and probability to students preparing for JEE Main, JEE Advanced and the Science Olympiads at VigyanPath Classes in Gwalior, where I also coordinate a team of 10 to 15 people.

By training I am a computer scientist. I hold an M.Tech from MNIT Jaipur, published research on detecting lung cancer in CT scans with deep learning, and worked as a data scientist at Micron Technology on data from semiconductor fabs.

I left industry for the classroom because mathematics was the part of machine learning I loved most. I never stopped building, though. The OMR checker that grades our exams, the paper generator, the online test portal and the virtual science lab are all things I wrote for my students.

2The journey so far

Two threads run through it. Yellow marks the classroom, blue marks engineering and research.

  1. 2016to 2020

    B.E. in Information Technology

    Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior

    Four years of engineering in my home city, where programming and mathematics first started to feel like the same subject. Graduated with first division.

  2. 2020to 2022

    M.Tech at MNIT Jaipur

    Computer Engineering and Information Security, Department of Computer Science and Engineering

    My thesis, supervised by Dr. Lavika Goel, asked whether a deep-learning detector could find lung cancer in CT scans more reliably if its weights were tuned by a nature-inspired optimiser. It became my first published paper.

  3. 2022alongside the M.Tech

    Teaching online, one PYQ at a time

    Teaching ran next to my own studies. This is a live class from February 2022, working through previous-year JEE questions on area under curves.

  4. 2022Aug to May 2023

    Data Scientist II at Micron Technology

    I worked with real-time data from fab tools across photolithography, etch, deposition and metrology. I built a system that turns machine data into ready-made analytical presentations, and owned a tool-utilisation project from the BigQuery pipelines and the models to the Angular front end.

  5. 2023May to today

    Back to Gwalior, back to the board

    Advanced Mathematics Faculty and Coordinator, VigyanPath Classes

    I came home to teach full time. I write my own question sheets and lecture decks, set and grade the exams, and lead a team of 10 to 15. The engineer in me automated what could be automated, so more of my time goes to students.

OMR grading systemScanned answer sheets in, audited results out.Python, computer vision, LaTeX
  • Reads scanned PDFs of JEE-style OMR sheets, finds the four corner marks and corrects the perspective of every page.
  • Scores each bubble from how filled it is, and understands a struck-out bubble.
  • Anything it is unsure about goes to a review queue with an image crop, so a person makes the final call.
  • Produces an audit file, an annotated copy of every sheet and typeset result PDFs. Used on real exam batches at the institute.

  • Exports a styled PDF and a Word file from the same source, from one command.

4Published research

A hybrid of modified YOLOv3 with BBO/EE optimizer for lung cancer detection

Lavika Goel and Satyansh Mishra
Multimedia Tools and Applications (Springer), 83(17), pages 52219 to 52251, 2024

Lung nodules are small, and a missed one matters. We took YOLOv3, a fast object detector, and let a nature-inspired optimiser search for better weights than ordinary training finds on its own. The model then marks suspected nodules on CT slices and separates cancer types from healthy scans.

92%accuracy on LUNA16
90.01%accuracy on LIDC-IDRI
